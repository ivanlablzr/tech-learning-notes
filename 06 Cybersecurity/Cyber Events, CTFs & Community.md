> [!abstract] What this note is
> Where to **compete, connect, and get recognised** in cyber — seasonal challenges with certificates, CTFs, conferences (weighted to 🇫🇷🇨🇭 near you), local meetups, and bug bounty. The [[IT Certifications and Learning Resources per Domain|certs note]] is for *credentials*; this is for **people and proof-of-skill**.

> [!tip] The three ways to get recognised — do all three
> Recognition in this field isn't a single track. It compounds when you:
> 1. **Compete** — CTFs & seasonal events give rankings, certificates, and something concrete to show.
> 2. **Contribute** — writeups, blog posts, a tool on GitHub, a bug bounty report. *Public work is your real CV* — the FR/CH market (Synacktiv, Intrinsec) hires on it. See [[Synthèse du marché des postes informatiques]].
> 3. **Show up** — in person. Jobs and mentorship come from meeting people at cons and meetups, not from a certificate PDF. This is the part most people skip, and it's the highest-leverage for someone building a network.
>
> Your advantage: you're near **Geneva/Lausanne + Grenoble**, three of Europe's best cyber scenes. Use it.

## 6. Turning it into recognition — the proof trail

Doing the events isn't enough; **capture the evidence**:

- **Writeups.** After every CTF/room, publish a short writeup (GitHub Pages / blog). This is the single highest-ROI habit — it proves skill, teaches you twice, and is what recruiters actually read.
- **Rankings & badges.** HTB rank, CTFtime team score, Root-Me score, TryHackMe streak — screenshot the milestones.
- **Certificates.** Advent of Cyber, Holiday Hack — file them; link from [[My Career]].
- **Speak.** A 15-min BSides or meetup talk on something you built puts you *on the map* faster than anything. Terrifying, worth it.
- **Ship a tool.** One useful thing on GitHub (from your [[Programming Learning Resources|project ideas]]) beats a wall of certs.

> [!note] Keep it honest and legal
> CTFs and bounty scope are your sandbox — **stay inside authorised targets**. The same discipline as your [[Portée et Règles d'engagement|rules of engagement]] at Decorec. Recognition built on out-of-scope testing destroys a reputation instantly in this field.
